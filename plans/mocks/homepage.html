Your household's data shouldn't live in someone else's business model

Hearth is open source under AGPL-3.0. Run it on a NAS in your cupboard and pay nothing, forever — not a trial, not a crippled tier, the whole thing.

Or let us host it, and the money goes to keeping the lights on rather than to an exit.

Nothing is held back

Self-hosted gets every feature. Things that cost money to run — AI, bank linking — use your own API keys instead of ours.

Leave whenever

Full data export is always available, including if you stop paying. Migrating between hosted and self-hosted goes both ways.

No investors to answer to

No ads, no data sales, no growth targets that turn into your problem. Subscriptions are the entire business model.

Two ways to run it

Same software either way. The only question is who keeps the server running.
