Questions people actually ask

What's the catch with the free version?

There isn't one, and I know how that sounds.

Self-hosted Hearth is the same software — every feature, no member limits, no nag screens. The difference is that things costing money to run (AI inference, bank data) use your own API keys rather than mine, and that you're the one restarting the server when it falls over.

If a feature ever gets withheld from self-hosted to push you toward Cloud, that's a bug. Open an issue about it.

Why one plan instead of tiers?

Because I couldn't come up with an honest reason to withhold a feature from you that costs me nothing to provide.

The only things charged separately are the ones with a real per-household bill attached. If that ever stops being true, the pricing page is lying and you should tell me.

Is the AI assistant limited?

There's a fair-use ceiling of around 150 conversations a month. For scale, a household that asks it something most days lands well under that.

It exists because AI genuinely costs money per message, and I'd rather cap the extreme end than promise "unlimited" and quietly break it later. If you do reach it, the assistant keeps working on a lighter model rather than stopping dead, and you'll see where you stand in Settings long before then.

Why is bank sync a separate charge?

Because it's the one feature with a real per-household bill attached. Aggregators charge per connected household, every month.

Keeping it separate means people importing a CSV once a month aren't subsidising it, and the base price stays where it is.

Why does paying monthly cost more?

Paying for the year up front is what lets a small operation cover its infrastructure bills ahead of time rather than hoping the month works out. The annual rate passes that back to you — 20% off.

Month-to-month exists because committing a year to software you've used for two weeks is a lot to ask. Start monthly, switch whenever; it takes effect at your next renewal.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your data stays put and export keeps working — permanently, regardless of subscription status. That isn't goodwill, it's written into how the access checks are built.

There's a grace period before the account locks, and you can export to a self-hosted install at any point and carry on for free.

Can I move between self-hosted and hosted?

Both directions. Export from one, import to the other. If you're moving a household with real history in it, email me and I'll help rather than leaving you to it.

Do you sell or train on my data?

No. No ads, no data sales, no investors expecting a return that would eventually require one of those. Subscriptions are the whole business model.
